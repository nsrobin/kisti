Rules Generated in code: mono, 44.1 kHz, under 30 KB, at most 35% volume. Never on keystrokes. iOS respects the silent switch and never stops the user's music. Sound off in Settings means fully silent. Vibration off disables every haptic. Reduce motion swaps rolls, the ring draw, sparks, shakes and pulses for instant changes or 150 ms fades.
